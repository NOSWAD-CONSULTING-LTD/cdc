CDC Manufacturing Knowledge Graph Data Flow

A4 landscape view of how product/process master data, manufacturing execution evidence, quality review, and CMC/QMS/regulatory evidence flow through the CDC graph.

How to read it: left-to-right shows the primary flow from governed context, to execution evidence, to quality disposition, to CMC/QMS/regulatory evidence.
Why it matters: the graph lets a stakeholder trace a manufacturing fact back to its source context and forward to the evidence package used for QA and regulatory discussion.
